Privacy Policy

Last Payment Chrome extension · effective October 8, 2026

What data the extension accesses

  • Google Sheets you pick. With the drive.file permission, the extension can only open the spreadsheets you select in the file picker. It reads the Deal ID, Last Payment Date and Last Payment Amount columns and writes to the two Last Payment columns.
  • LendTech CRM data. The extension reads a deal's Payment Activity from your LendTech CRM using the login session already open in your browser. It never asks for, stores or transmits your CRM password.

What is stored, and where

Only in your own browser: your last tenant and year choice, the sheet you picked per tenant, any tenants you add, and a short-lived Google access token for the current browser session. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the developer. There is no backend, no analytics and no tracking.

Sharing

Data is exchanged only between your browser, Google's Sheets and Drive APIs, and your LendTech CRM. It is not shared with, sold to or seen by anyone else.

Removing access

Remove the extension from chrome://extensions, and revoke its Google access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
