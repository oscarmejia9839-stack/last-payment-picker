Last Payment

A Chrome extension for MCA funders who track their deals in a Google Sheets master sheet.

What it does

For every deal listed on the month tabs of a master sheet, Last Payment reads the deal's Payment Activity from the funder's own LendTech CRM (using the person's existing CRM login) and fills in the Last Payment Date and Last Payment Amount columns. Nothing else on the sheet is touched.

What it asks Google for

Only the "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" permission (drive.file). The extension can read and write the sheets you pick in the file picker and nothing else in your Drive.

Who it is for

Private tool built for Vivian Capital Group and its partner funders. It is installed from a zip file, not from the Chrome Web Store.
